- Native-review the complete Russian and Uzbek body copy, navigation, forms, CTAs, metadata, and no-JavaScript fallbacks across all product and marketing routes.
- Give extra review to Uzbek dialect/code-switching language and regulated terminology on banking and debt-collection pages.
- Confirm that claims, pricing, free-trial wording, deployment/security statements, and consent language carry the same meaning in all three languages.
- Review localized competitor-comparison wording against the linked vendor sources at publication time.
- Translate and review the final customer testimonials, case studies, methodology, and team biographies after placeholders are replaced.
- Re-run the translation review whenever the English source copy or metadata changes.
