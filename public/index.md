# Syncall — a phone assistant for your customers

Syncall makes sales calls, follows up on overdue payments, and answers incoming calls. It uses data from your business systems during the conversation and speaks Uzbek and Russian, day and night.

[Try a conversation](https://www.syncallai.com/#live-demo) or [listen to call recordings](https://www.syncallai.com/#demo).

## What can it do?

- Sales calls: follow up on enquiries, check available offers using CRM data, and save the customer's needs and agreed next step.
- Debt collection: check the outstanding balance and recent payments, discuss the reason for a delay, and record an agreed payment date.
- Incoming calls: look up the customer's payment, order, or service status, explain the issue, and create a support request.

We connect the assistant to the customer records and billing data it needs. You try it on your call examples before it starts handling customer calls.

## Other ways Syncall can help

- [Check staff conversations](https://www.syncallai.com/analytics): find mistakes and unanswered questions in call recordings.
- [Reply to messages](https://www.syncallai.com/chatbots): answer common questions on your website and in Telegram.
- [Turn audio into text](https://www.syncallai.com/cabinet/stt) or [text into speech](https://www.syncallai.com/cabinet/tts): sign in to use Syncall Studio.
